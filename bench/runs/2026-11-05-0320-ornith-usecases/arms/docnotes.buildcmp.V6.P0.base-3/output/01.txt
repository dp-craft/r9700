TOPIC: A stew675 rdna-boosts patch for llama.cpp was benchmarked versus stock Vulkan and ROCm builds for prefill/decode throughput on an AMD R9700.
VERDICT: The patch boosts prefill throughput by +14-20% on ROCm while leaving decode flat, but is essentially flat and regresses ~12% on Vulkan at the largest prompt. For prefill-heavy workloads ROCm+stew675 wins; for decode-heavy workloads Vulkan still leads.
