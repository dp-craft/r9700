GOAL: Validate whether three transferred runner-simplify commits changed outcomes, compare local models against each other and against the production Claude path, on two code-generation workloads (047 phase-1 and 045 T010).
RESULT: The harness change was outcome-neutral (wall difference ~102 s, t=0.80), and between-block variance is the dominant noise term — larger than any model or arm difference — so rankings built on one block or one sample are unsafe. Best local defaults are complementary (Brian6145/27B-Q4_K_M for statement-driven work, unsloth/35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M for contract/test-bearing work), and ThinkingCap-27B-Q6_K_L posted the best operational numbers yet overwrote four working translations in 4 of 5 samples, so gate-green is not a quality proxy.
