TOPIC: At a ~132.9k-token prompt, three axes were tested against deterministic TS% and a blind judge: the Qwen3.6-27B quant ladder, the 4096/16384/unlimited reasoning budget, and the 35B-A3B sampling sweep across 210 graded replies on one AMD R9700 box.
VERDICT: The three axes each resolve to one answer — the quant ladder is a bounded null with no precision gain worth paying for, budget above ~4k buys nothing on the dense 27B (and one unlimited reply ran away), and the vendor's temp-0.6 coding recipe is the best sampling setting. Cleanliness (lint/types) not logic is the quality wall, presence_penalty loses decode without a clear cause, but unexplained GTT spill on a no-swap box blocks unattended use.
