TOPIC: A stock-vs-patched build comparison of the stew675 rdna-boosts patch on an AMD R9700, measuring prefill and decode throughput under Vulkan and ROCm backends.
VERDICT: The stew675 patch boosts ROCm prefill by +14 to +20% but is flat or slightly worse on Vulkan; decode is unaffected on either backend. So ROCm+patch suits prefill-heavy workloads and Vulkan suits decode-heavy ones.
CONFIG: Qwen3.8-27B-UD-Q4_K_XL; llama.cpp builds b10969 (Vulkan stock), b10909 (ROCm stock), b16 (stew675-patched); ROCm 7.8.0 / Mesa 26.2.2; -ub 2048, -b 4096, -fa on, CTK/CTV q8_0, NGL 99; PG shapes, REPS=1, no warmup
