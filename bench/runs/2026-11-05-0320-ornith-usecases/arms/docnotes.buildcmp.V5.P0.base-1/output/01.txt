TOPIC: Compared stock vs stew675-patched llama.cpp builds on the AMD R9700 (gfx1201) under ROCm and Vulkan via llama-bench PG shapes to measure Qwen3.8-27B prefill and decode throughput.
VERDICT: The patch raises ROCm prefill throughput by about 14-20% with decode unchanged, making ROCm the fastest prefill config measured; on Vulkan it stays flat or slightly worse (-12% at the largest prompt). The gain is ROCm-only because the kernel optimizations target ROCm/HIP paths rather than Vulkan shaders.
CONFIG: Qwen3.8-27B-UD-Q4_K_XL; llama.cpp stock (b10969 Vulkan, b10909 ROCm) and stew675-patched (b16) on ROCm 7.8.0 / Mesa Vulkan 26.2.2; UB=2048, BATCH=4096, CTK/CTV=q8_0, FA=on, NGL=99, PG shapes (128,64) to (32768,2048), REPS=1, --no-warmup
