TOPIC: Compared the stew675 rdna-boosts patched llama.cpp build against stock builds on an AMD R9700 (gfx1201), measuring prefill and decode tok/s across Vulkan and ROCm backends.
VERDICT: The patch's benefit is ROCm-only, adding +14-20% prefill throughput with decode unchanged and making stew675-ROCm the fastest prefill config measured. On Vulkan it is flat to slightly worse (-1% to -3%, plus a ~12% regression at the 32768 prompt), so there is no cross-backend benefit.
