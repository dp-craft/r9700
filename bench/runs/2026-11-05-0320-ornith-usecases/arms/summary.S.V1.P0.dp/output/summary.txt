GOAL: Benchmark the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B model on an AMD Radeon AI PRO R9700 (Vulkan/ROCm) at its native 262144 RoPE ceiling to characterize prefill/decode decay with depth, and the effects of MTP speculative decode, q8_0 KV cache, and parallel throughput at the context limit.
RESULT: f16 KV ran cleanly to ~193K real tokens (200K cap) with no OOM on both MTP off and on, confirming VRAM is not the ceiling — the RoPE cap is. But MTP is a net loss for deep-prefill/short-decode requests (-17 s wall at 200K), q8_0 KV is catastrophic at depth and hard-rejected, and parallel 32K prompts are prefill-bound (concurrency adds TTFT, not throughput); the winning config is Vulkan + f16 with MTP on only when generation dominates prefill.

  -17 s | wall-clock net loss of MTP on vs off at 200K deep prompt (f16 KV, 158 -> 175 s)
  +35.1% | MTP decode speedup growing with depth @200K on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (f16 KV, vs 61.0 -> 82.4 tok/s)
  -59% | prefill tok/s decay 8K->200K on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (f16 KV, MTP off, 3077 -> 1254)
  49 s | TTFT p95 for 4 parallel 32K prompts vs 24 s @np2 — aggregate flat (5.5 -> 6.0 tok/s)
CAVEAT: The parallel finding is under-sampled at depth because the agentic prompts EOS after only 16-256 tokens, so early-EOS under-measures aggregate decode scaling; VRAM was also not re-sampled this run (the "no OOM" claim is served-path evidence and the ~21 KiB/tok figure is prior-MEASURED).
