TOPIC: A/B benchmarked the stew675 rdna-boosts patch (llama.cpp b16) against stock llama.cpp builds on Vulkan and ROCm via llama-bench PG shapes on an AMD R9700.
VERDICT: The patch yields +14-20% prefill throughput on ROCm but is flat-to-worse on Vulkan, while decode is unchanged on either backend. The prefill gain grows with prompt size, so ROCm+stew675 suits prefill-heavy workloads and Vulkan suits decode-heavy ones.
