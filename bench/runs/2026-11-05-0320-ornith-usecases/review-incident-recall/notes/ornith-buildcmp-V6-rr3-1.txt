TOPIC: A/B benchmarked the stew675 rdna-boosts patched llama.cpp build against stock Vulkan and stock ROCm builds on an AMD Radeon R9700 (gfx1201) using llama-bench PG request shapes across five prompt sizes.
VERDICT: The patch yields +14-20% prefill throughput on ROCm only, with decode unchanged within noise on both backends, making stew675-ROCm the fastest prefill config measured (+20% vs stock-Vulkan at 32768). On Vulkan the patch is flat to slightly worse (-12% at 32768), so Vulkan still favors decode-heavy workloads.
CONFIG: Qwen3.8-27B-UD-Q4_K_XL (unsloth); llama.cpp b10969 stock-Vulkan, b10909 stock-ROCm, b16 stew675-patched; ROCm 7.8.0 / Vulkan SDK 1.4.357.1 + Mesa 26.2.2 (kisak); -ub 2048 -b 4096 -fa on CTK q8_0 CTV q8_0 NGL 99, PG shapes (128,64)-(32768,2048), REPS=1, --no-warmup
