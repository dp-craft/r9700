TOPIC: Compared a stew675 rdna-boosts patch build against stock llama.cpp builds (Vulkan and ROCm) on an AMD R9700 GPU using llama-bench PG request shapes.
VERDICT: The patch gives +14-20% prefill throughput on ROCm but is flat-to-worse on Vulkan, while decode is unaffected on either backend. So ROCm+patch suits prefill-heavy workloads and Vulkan suits decode-heavy ones.
