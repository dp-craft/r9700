TOPIC: Benchmarked Ornith 1.5-35B and Qwen 27B/35B decode and prefill throughput on native Radiance vs Sly (vLLM) on an R9700 GPU at a 210 W power cap.
VERDICT: Ornith 1.5-35B on native Radiance with MTP K=3 is about 2-2.5x faster than Qwen3.8-27B on Sly, decoding ~220 t/s and prefilling >13k t/s at 10k. On Sly it is much slower, the EMULATION MoE backend drops the 35B to ~16 t/s, MTP will not load, and Radiance's default headroom crashed the desktop.
CONFIG: Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B w4a8 on native Radiance 1.16.0; Qwen3.8-27B (MXFP4/DFlash2) and Qwen3.5-35B on Sly vLLM 1.9.1; MTP or DFlash drafting, fp8 KV cache, temperature 1.0, ctx 262144
