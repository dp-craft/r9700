TOPIC: Diagnosis and fix of random HSA_STATUS_ERROR_MEMORY_FAULT during vLLM Sly radiance MXFP4 + DFlash2 serving on the R9700, traced to kernel-argument placement.
VERDICT: Setting HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0 (kernel args in host memory) removed the memory faults in 3 of 3 boots with 4 Claude turns each, at full speed, and it is now the default in the vLLM profiles. The evidence is strong but not final: only 3 boots, and the fix has not been confirmed on kernel 7.0.0-38.
CONFIG: vLLM via ghcr.io/slybase/vllm-sly-radiance:1.6.0-rocm10.1 (vLLM 0.31.0); amd/Qwen3.8-27B-Quark-AWQ-MXFP4 with DFlash2 drafter syvai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2-W4A16, k=7, greedy; HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0; max-model-len 180000; max-num-seqs 1; util 0.90; kv fp8; prefix caching on; kernel 7.0.0-34 for fix runs.
