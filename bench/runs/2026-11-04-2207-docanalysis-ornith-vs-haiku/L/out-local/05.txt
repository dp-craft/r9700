TOPIC: diagnosed and solved a recurring HSA_STATUS_ERROR_MEMORY_FAULT on vLLM Sly radiance MXFP4 with DFlash on the R9700, traced to a corrupted device-memory kernel-argument pointer and fixed with HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0.
VERDICT: The fault (a valid-pointer address with bit 44 cleared) is fixed by HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0, giving three clean boots x four Claude turns with zero faults at unchanged speed (~111-150 tok/s decode, ~2.4-3.0k tok/s prefill); the fix is now default in every vLLM profile but still needs a gate run on kernel 7.0.0-38.
CONFIG: amd/Qwen3.8-27B-Quark-AWQ-MXFP4 plus syvai Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2-W4A16 drafter k=7; engine ghcr.io/slybase/vllm-sly-radiance 1.6.0 (vLLM 0.31.0) with the RADIANCE_MXFP4_ knobs and HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0; max-model-len 180000, fp8 KV, R4D_HYBRID, cudagraph FULL_DECODE_ONLY; kernel 7.0.0-34
