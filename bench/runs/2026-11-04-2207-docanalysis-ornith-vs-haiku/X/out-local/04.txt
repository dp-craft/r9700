TOPIC: Benchmarked Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B on native Radiance vs Sly (plus Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3.5-35B) decode throughput at 10k/50k/100k on one R9700, where MTP only loads on native Radiance.
VERDICT: Ornith-1.5 on native Radiance with its own MTP (K=3) is the fastest 35B here at ~220/205/175 t/s decode and about 2.1-2.5x the Qwen3.8-27B on Sly, because Radiance's per-step time beats Sly's while draft acceptance is equal. On Sly the same 35B falls to the slow EMULATION MoE backend (about 16 t/s) and its MTP will not load on any usable backend; coding quality is untested and Radiance's default VRAM headroom crashed the desktop.
