TOPIC: Compared a stew675 rdna-boosts patch build against stock llama.cpp builds on Vulkan and ROCm via llama-bench PG shapes on Qwen3.8-27B, finding the prefill benefit is ROCm-only.
VERDICT: The stew675 rdna-boosts patch gives +14-20% prefill throughput on ROCm at no decode cost but is flat (and slightly worse at 32768) on Vulkan, so its benefit is ROCm-only because it targets ROCm-specific WMMA kernel paths. For prefill-heavy workloads stew675-ROCm is fastest; for decode-heavy workloads Vulkan still wins, while a ~12% Vulkan prefill regression at the largest shape stays unexplained.
