TOPIC: Diagnosed and solved a random HSA_STATUS_ERROR_MEMORY_FAULT on the R9700 in Sly radiance vLLM MXFP4 plus DFlash, caused by a corrupted kernel-argument pointer placed in device memory; fixed with HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0.
VERDICT: A random HSA_STATUS_ERROR_MEMORY_FAULT that hit most boots of Sly radiance MXFP4 + DFlash traces to a corrupted kernel-argument pointer (bit 44 cleared, faults at 0x6 while buffers live at 0x7), and it disappears with HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0 keeping kernel args in host memory — 3/3 boots times 4 turns clean at full speed. The fix is now the vLLM default; which path flips bit 44 (PCIe link, BAR write-combining, or firmware) stays unestablished.
CONFIG: Sly radiance vLLM 1.5.1/1.6.0 (vLLM 0.31.0), ROCm 10.1; amd/Qwen3.8-27B-Quark-AWQ-MXFP4 + syvai Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2 k=7; --max-model-len 180000, fp8 KV, R4D_HYBRID, gpu-util 0.90; env HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG=0 (fix); kernel 7.0.0-34/38
